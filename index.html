CPython compiled to WebAssembly via Pyodide, running inside this browser tab. No server is involved.

Your code runs on your own CPU and never leaves your device. The runtime (~10 MB) is downloaded once and then cached by the browser.

Most of the standard library is available; threads, subprocesses and raw sockets are not.
